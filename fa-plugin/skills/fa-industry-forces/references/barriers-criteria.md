# Entry Barriers and Government Protection — India Reference

## Entry Barrier Criteria by Type

### Capital Intensity Barriers

**High barrier (>₹500 cr to reach competitive scale):**

- Steel, cement, aluminum, refining, petrochemicals
- Power generation (thermal/solar/hydro at scale)
- Telecom (spectrum + towers + fiber network)
- Automotive OEM manufacturing
- Airport / port infrastructure

**Medium barrier (₹50-500 cr):**

- Wires and cables manufacturing
- Paints manufacturing
- Pharmaceutical formulation plant (non-sterile)
- NBFC / HFC (RBI minimum capital norms)
- Mid-size industrial components

**Low barrier (<₹50 cr):**

- Software / SaaS businesses
- Trading companies
- Light assembly manufacturing
- Many consumer service businesses

---

### Regulatory License Barriers

**Very strong (license supply strictly limited):**

- Banking license (Scheduled Commercial Bank) — RBI issues rarely
- Insurance license (Life + General) — IRDAI strict criteria
- Telecom spectrum — auction-based, limited spectrum available
- National securities exchange / depository (SEBI)
- Nuclear power

**Strong (approval process lengthy and costly):**

- USFDA approval for pharmaceutical plant (US exports)
- WHO-GMP certification for pharma (export markets)
- IRDAI / RBI approval for NBFCs and HFCs
- DCGI clinical trial approvals for drug launches
- Defense manufacturing license (DPP)
- Aviation operator certificate

**Moderate (achievable but takes 1-3 years):**

- Food Safety and Standards Authority (FSSAI) for food products
- BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) certification for many products
- Environmental clearance for new manufacturing plants
- Drug manufacturing license (State FDA)

## Import Duty Reference by Relevant Sector

Import duties create a price umbrella protecting domestic manufacturers from cheaper foreign imports.


| Sector                      | Applicable Duty                              | Protection Level      |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Wires & Cables              | 15-25% basic customs duty                    | High                  |
| Paints / Coatings           | 10-15%                                       | Moderate              |
| Dairy products              | 30-60% (sector specific)                     | High                  |
| FMCG / Packaged Food        | 10-40% depending on product                  | Moderate-High         |
| Solar panels (imported)     | 40% ALMM + BCD                               | Very High (post-2022) |
| Auto components             | 5-15%                                        | Moderate              |
| Pharmaceutical APIs         | 0-10% (India encourages API imports for now) | Low-Moderate          |
| Pharmaceutical formulations | 10%                                          | Low                   |
| Electronic components       | 0-20%                                        | Low-Moderate          |


**Important:** Import duty rates change with Union Budgets. Always verify current rates for specific HS codes from CBIC (cbic.gov.in) if precision is needed.

## Make-in-India / Aatmanirbhar Bharat Beneficiaries

The following are explicit focus sectors where government policy explicitly favors domestic manufacturers:

1. **Defense**: DRDO, DPP, positive indigenization lists (import substitution mandate)
2. **Semiconductors and Electronics**: CHIPS India, PLI for mobile manufacturing
3. **Renewable Energy**: Domestic content requirements for solar equipment
4. **Pharmaceuticals**: API Park scheme, bulk drug PLI
5. **Textiles**: PM-MITRA (Mega Integrated Textile Region and Apparel)
6. **Chemicals**: Specialty chemicals have been a beneficiary of China+1 sourcing by global companies

## Competitive Dynamics Warning Signs

### Industry-Level Warning Signs

- Top 3 players combined market share declining (new entrants gaining)
- EBITDA margins declining industry-wide for 3+ consecutive years (not just one player)
- Capacity utilization falling below 70% industry-wide (demand lagging supply)
- Industry CAGR decelerating meaningfully vs prior 5-year period

### Company-Specific Warning Signs (for context)

- Company losing market share despite industry growth
- EBITDA margins falling while peers' are stable or improving
- Relying increasingly on government orders as private demand weakens
- Management starting to discuss diversification away from core business (often signals core is struggling)
